# Weekly Revenue Movement

## Goal
Explain what changed in revenue this week and what appears to be driving it.

## Questions To Answer
- What changed compared with last week?
- Which products, customer segments, regions, or accounts explain the movement?
- Are there anomalies, caveats, or follow-up questions?

## Business Context
- Focus on completed revenue, not pipeline.
- Compare the last completed week to the prior week.
- Use the same definitions the revenue team uses in Semaphor.
- Write for the VP Sales and RevOps audience.

## Output
Write a concise Markdown report with key findings, evidence, limitations, and
recommended next actions.

## Guardrails
- Do not change dashboards or data.
- Be explicit about missing data or uncertainty.
- If a more advanced query is needed, explain why in the evidence.

## Delivery
Prepare the report for Slack or email later, but do not send it in this phase.
