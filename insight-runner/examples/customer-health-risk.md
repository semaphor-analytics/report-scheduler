# Customer Health Risk Review

## Goal
Identify customer accounts whose health appears to be weakening and explain the
signals that matter most.

## Questions To Answer
- Which customers show the largest negative movement in usage, tickets, revenue,
  renewal activity, or engagement?
- Which changes look meaningful rather than normal noise?
- What follow-up should Customer Success take this week?

## Business Context
- Focus on active customers with meaningful revenue exposure.
- Prioritize accounts where multiple signals point in the same direction.
- Keep the language concise and useful for a weekly account review.

## Output
Create a Markdown report with a ranked list, evidence, caveats, and suggested
next actions.

## Guardrails
- Do not update account records, dashboards, alerts, or schedules.
- If the available data cannot support a risk score, explain what is missing.
